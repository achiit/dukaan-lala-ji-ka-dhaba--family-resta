Our story

A dhaba where the whole family fits at one table

Years ago my younger brother and I used to eat at roadside dhabas on long drives and wish for one place in Varanasi that served proper home-style food with a big family-sized table. So we opened one on Lanka Road.

The Lala Ji Thali came from my mother's kitchen: dal fry, paneer, mixed veg, jeera rice, four hot rotis, and a little meetha achar on the side. I wanted every plate to feel like a meal at home.

Come with your parents, your kids or a full group of friends. We will make sure everyone is fed well.

  • Four cuisines under one roof, so nobody argues about where to eat
  • A full thali for ₹250 with 4 butter rotis
  • Freshly prepared curries, never reheated leftovers
  • Easy family seating, near Bhelupur Chauraha
  • Dine in, take away, or order on Zomato and Swiggy
